Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026 · App: Stone Tech OS (in.vedoravision.stonetechos.app) · Publisher: Vedora Vision

Privacy Commitment: Stone Tech OS is an enterprise operations and enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform for stone fabrication and construction operations developed by Vedora Vision. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or commercial data to advertising networks or third-party data brokers.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Vedora Vision ("we", "our", or "us") collects, stores, protects, and discloses information when you use the Stone Tech OS (STOS) web application (https://erp.stonetech.in) and native Android mobile application (in.vedoravision.stonetechos.app).

2. Information We Collect

To provide enterprise management, estimation, workflow intelligence, and logistics features, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Account & Identity Information: Name, work email address, mobile number, assigned job role, and organization credentials when an account is provisioned for you.
  • Business & Operational Records: Customers, vendors, projects, quotations, sales orders, purchase transport manifests, inventory movement records, and delivery challans created within your organization.
  • Payment & Accounting Metadata: Invoice references, payment receipt amounts, payment mode references (e.g., UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque), and allocation details recorded by authorized finance personnel. We do not store sensitive credit card numbers or banking PINs.
  • Media & Documents (Optional User-Initiated Uploads): Photos of stone slabs, proof of material delivery, and PDF documents you explicitly select and upload to project records or dispatch entries.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, network connectivity status, and crash reports to ensure platform stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information solely for legitimate enterprise and operational business functions:

  • To facilitate the end-to-end stone fabrication lifecycle: Enquiry → Estimate → Quotation → Sales Order → Manufacturing → Dispatch → Invoicing → Ledger.
  • To deliver realtime operational notifications (such as dispatch challan alerts, quote approvals, and payment confirmations) to authorized organization members.
  • To enable user-initiated communication, such as sending order and ledger updates to customers via WhatsApp or email upon explicit employee action.
  • To monitor system performance, enforce role-based access security, and debug errors.

4. Device Permissions & Purpose

🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used solely to display timely operational heads-up banners on your mobile device (e.g., material dispatched, quote approved, payment recorded). You can control or revoke this permission anytime in your device settings.

📷 Camera & Storage / Photos (Optional)

Only accessed when you explicitly choose to take a photo or attach a document (e.g., capturing a proof-of-dispatch challan, slab inspection photo, or invoice receipt). The app never scans your background photos or gallery without your direct selection.

5. Data Security & Storage

We adhere to industry-standard data protection practices:

  • Database Security: All data is stored in PostgreSQL infrastructure provided by Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) policies enforced on all tables.
  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Stored records and attachments are encrypted at rest.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Access to sensitive financial ledgers and administrative configurations is restricted strictly to designated organizational roles.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with verified technical infrastructure providers strictly necessary to deliver the service:

  • Database & Authentication: Supabase (hosted on secure cloud infrastructure).
  • Hosting & Edge Delivery: Cloudflare Workers.
  • Transactional Alerts & Delivery: Resend (transactional email) and WhatsApp Business API / Web Intent (for user-triggered customer notifications).
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable laws, court orders, or governmental regulations.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain operational data for as long as your organization maintains an active subscription with Stone Tech OS. You or your organization administrator may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To request data or account deletion, contact our Data Privacy Officer at stonetech.ahmedabad@gmail.com. Upon verification, requested personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

8. Children’s Privacy

Stone Tech OS is an enterprise workplace management application designed exclusively for commercial operations and adult professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect evolving operational practices or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
